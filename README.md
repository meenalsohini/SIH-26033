## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

We propose a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

The platform allows farmers to register, upload their products, mention quantities and prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.

The main features of our solution are:

Farmer and buyer registration.

Online agricultural product listing.

Direct buying and selling.

AI-based demand forecasting.

Market price information.

Logistics and transportation support.

Order tracking and digital payment facilities.

2. How It Addresses the Problem

Reduces the dependency on multiple intermediaries.

Helps farmers connect directly with consumers and bulk buyers.

Provides better selling opportunities for farmers.

Helps consumers purchase products at reasonable prices.

Provides transportation support for product delivery.

Uses AI to understand market demand and avoid excess supply.

Improves transparency in agricultural trading.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

Combines a digital marketplace with AI-based demand forecasting.

Provides direct communication between farmers and buyers.

Offers smart transportation and route optimization.

Provides transparent product details and pricing.

Supports regional languages for easy access by farmers.

Brings farmers, consumers, and bulk buyers onto one platform.

The implementation process includes:

Farmer and buyer registration.

Collecting farmer and product information.

Uploading agricultural products to the platform.

Displaying available products to buyers.

Allowing buyers to search and place orders.

Using AI to forecast product demand.

Planning transportation and delivery routes.

Delivering products to buyers.

Processing payments to farmers.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

Technical Feasibility:
The platform can be developed using commonly available technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and MySQL.

Economic Feasibility:
Using open-source software and digital services can reduce development costs.

Operational Feasibility:
Farmers and buyers can access the platform through smartphones and computers.

Scalability:
The platform can be expanded to support farmers, consumers, and bulk buyers across different regions.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

Lack of digital knowledge among some farmers.

Poor internet connectivity in rural areas.

Difficulty in arranging transportation.

Changes in agricultural market prices.

Maintaining product quality.

Building trust between farmers and buyers.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

Support regional languages.

Provide easy registration and product uploading.

Connect with local transportation providers.

Regularly update market price information.

Introduce buyer and seller verification.

Provide product ratings and feedback facilities.
