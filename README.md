## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Farmers often depend on multiple middlemen to sell their agricultural products. This reduces their earnings and increases the prices paid by consumers. There is also a lack of proper transportation and market information.

The proposed solution is to create a digital marketplace that directly connects farmers with consumers and bulk buyers.

Proposed Solution

1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

We propose a digital platform that connects farmers, FPOs, consumers and bulk buyers directly. Farmers can upload their products, mention quantities and set prices. Buyers can view available products and purchase them directly.

The platform also provides transportation support and AI-based demand forecasting to improve the selling and delivery process.

2. How It Addresses the Problem

Reduces the need for unnecessary middlemen.

Helps farmers sell products directly to buyers.

Provides fair prices for farmers.

Helps consumers purchase products at lower prices.

Improves transportation and delivery facilities.

Provides market and demand information.

3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

Direct connection between farmers and buyers.

AI-based demand prediction.

Smart transportation and route optimization.

Easy-to-use digital marketplace.

Transparent product prices.

Supports both small farmers and bulk buyers.
## Technical Approach
Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Backend: Python.

Database: MySQL.

AI/ML: Demand forecasting and prediction.

Maps: GPS and map integration for delivery tracking.

Cloud: Cloud storage and hosting.

2. Methodology and Process for Implementation

Farmers register on the platform.

Farmers upload their agricultural products.

Buyers browse and select products.

Buyers place orders directly with farmers.

The system calculates delivery requirements.

AI predicts product demand and helps optimize delivery routes.

Products are transported to buyers.

Buyers receive their orders.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

 The proposed platform is practical and can be developed using existing technologies. Smartphones and internet access can help farmers and buyers use the platform easily.

It can be implemented step by step, starting with a small group of farmers and gradually expanding to different locations.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

Lack of digital knowledge among some farmers.

Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.

Transportation and delivery difficulties.

Maintaining product quality.

Building trust between farmers and buyers.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

Offer regional language support.

Provide training and guidance to farmers.

Partner with local transportation services.

Introduce product quality verification.
